Christmas Bird Count tools

CBC Circle Manager

For a count's compiler. Keeps the roster, area assignments, and results for one or more count circles, in a database that stays in your browser. Share an area from it to send a counter a link or QR code for CBC Scout.

CBC Scout

For field counters. Open the area link your compiler sends, and it shows your area, where you are, and what's been seen nearby, and records your party's hours and miles. Install it on your phone.
